# Stackoverflow-Job-Openings-and-Labor-Turnover-Survey
This is a project my colleagues and I worked on in our graduate school investingating solutions for a company named SkillzGuide who provides analytical job searching solutions to students across the United States. The ask was to look over the popularity of programming language in the US. Using the Stackoverflow and JOLTS(Job Opening and Labor Turnover) dataset, we've uncovered trends and answered the business question. 
*(Stackoverflow is a community for developers looking to discuss tech related topics and Jolts is a Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey that provides information and job openings around the US.)
